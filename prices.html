Commission Prices

Choose Your Art Style!

Looking for a cute and personalized illustration? Check out my commission options below and choose the style that suits your idea best!

Headshot – IDR 40K

A detailed illustration focusing on your character's head and face. Perfect for profile pictures, avatars, or simply capturing your character's expression!

Half Body – IDR 50K

An illustration featuring your character from the waist up. Great for showing off their outfit, pose, and personality with more details.

Chibi – IDR 35K

A cute illustration of your character in an adorable chibi style, with a small body and an expressive face. Perfect for stickers, gifts, or profile pictures!

Custom Requests

Have a different idea in mind? Custom requests are welcome! Prices may vary depending on the complexity, details, and specific requirements of your artwork. Feel free to contact me to discuss your ideas and get a personalized price estimate.
