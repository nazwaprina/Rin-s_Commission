Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a Commission?

A commission is a custom artwork created based on your personal request. You can share your ideas, references, and preferences, and I'll turn them into a unique illustration just for you.

2. What Kind of Artworks Can I Order?

I offer several illustration options, including chibi, half-body, and full-body artwork. You can check the Commission Prices page to see the available packages.

3. How Do I Place an Order?

You can place an order by visiting the Order Form page and filling in the required information. Don't forget to include your artwork description and reference images!

4. How Long Does It Take to Finish an Artwork?

The completion time depends on the complexity of the artwork and the current commission queue. I'll provide an estimated completion time after reviewing your request.

5. Can I Request Revisions?

Yes! You can request revisions during the drawing process. However, major changes to the original concept may require additional discussion.

6. How Will I Receive My Artwork?

Your finished artwork will be delivered as a digital file. The final file will be sent through the contact method agreed upon during the ordering process.

7. Can I Use the Artwork for Commercial Purposes?

The commission is intended for personal use unless commercial usage has been discussed and agreed upon beforehand.

Still Have Questions?

If you have other questions or need more information, feel free to visit the Contact page. I'll be happy to help you with your commission!
