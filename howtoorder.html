How to Order

Ready to Commission Your Artwork?

Ordering a custom artwork from Rin's Commission is easy! Just follow these simple steps to bring your ideas to life. Whether you want a cute character illustration, a special gift, or an artwork of your favorite character, I'm here to help!

Step 1 – Choose Your Commission Package

Take a look at the available commission packages on the Commission Prices page. Choose the type of artwork that best suits your needs, such as chibi, half body, or full body.

Step 2 – Prepare Your References

Prepare any reference images or descriptions that can help me understand your idea. You can include details about the character's appearance, pose, outfit, expression, colors, and preferred background.

Step 3 – Fill Out the Order Form

Go to the Order Form page and fill in the required information. Make sure your contact details and artwork descriptions are correct so I can understand your commission request clearly.

Step 4 – Wait for Confirmation

After submitting your order, please wait for confirmation. I will review your request and contact you to discuss the details, availability, and payment information.

Step 5 – Let the Artwork Begin!

Once everything is confirmed, I'll start working on your artwork. I'll keep you updated throughout the process. Thank you for trusting me with your creative ideas!
